# 👾 BRICK DEFENSE 🕹

## 👀 LORE
You are a paddle who mercilessly murdered hundreds, even thousands of bricks with your ball.
But now, the bricks are revolting, they are tired of being killed, and are now invading you.
Defeat all the bricks with your upgrades and perks to win!

## 🎮 CONTROLS
A and D to move left and right (or arrow keys)

# 🧱 BRICKS
Bricks are the obstacle you have to face, they slowly go down until they reach the bottom.

You have to prevent that from happening.

## 🎲 BRICK CLASSES
### ⬜ Default
  These bricks are regular bricks, without any mutations
  
  Doesn't Stack, Basic
### 💪 Reinforcement
  Adds extra health to the bricks
  
  Stacks up infinitely
### 🚤 Quick 
  These bricks can go faster
  
  Stacks up to Quick VII
### 🪙 Gold
  Gives extra money when destroyed
  
  Stacks up to Gold XV
### 👻 Ghost
  Has the ability to hide for a bit, allowing the balls to phase through it without losing HP
  
  Stacks up to Ghost III
### 🧟 Necromancer
  Can summon other bricks, being a large threat

  Stacks up to Necromancer V
### 🪤 Trap
  Traps the balls temporarily disabling them until a certain amount of time

  Stacks up to Trap VI
### 💥 Explosive
  An explosive brick that can break any balls in it's range once blown up

  Stacks up to Explosive III
### 🐜 Mini
  A tiny brick making it harder for balls to hit

  Stacks up to Mini IV
### 🚧 Spike
  A brick that can spike up breaking balls

  Stacks up to Spike III
### 🔫 Blaster
  A brick that can shoot at the turret, temporaly disabling it

  Stacks up to Blaster V
### 🕺 Leader
  A brick that gives a random mutation bonus to surrounding bricks until destroyed

  Stacks up infinitely
### 🕳 Teleporter
  Can teleport surrounding bricks downwards

  Stacks up to Teleporter III
### 👹 Giga
  These bricks are the bosses, big with high hp
  
  Doesn't Stack, Giga

# ⚽ BALLS
Balls are what you use to break the bricks.

Don't let them fall, then you would have no balls to defend the bricks!

x#.# means upgrade cost multiplier
